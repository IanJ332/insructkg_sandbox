# Publication and ownership notice

This repository publishes the original SIIP integration code and explicitly synthetic fixtures under the project owner's authorization. No blanket open-source license has been assigned: the project owner/institution must determine the appropriate license and contributor rights before one is added.

Upstream InstructKG and PrairieLearn repositories are retrieved at recorded commits by setup scripts. Their source, PDFs, original research outputs and model weights are not vendored or republished here. They retain their own authorship and licensing terms. Repository availability does not establish redistribution rights for educational materials. Local course text, original graph evidence, model outputs, raw PL exports, logs and recordings are excluded from publication.

Original synthetic contract fixtures are clearly labeled as such. They test adapters and CI; they are not extracted knowledge graphs, actual course lectures or evidence of live inference. See docs/engineering/SECURITY_AUDIT.md for the publication boundary and audit evidence.
